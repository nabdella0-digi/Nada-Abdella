# Egypt Quest 🇪🇬

### Interactive Tourism Mobile App

**Project Overview**

Egypt Quest is an interactive tourism mobile application designed to encourage users to explore Egypt through technology, adventure, and gamification.

**My Role: Digital Marketing Team Member**

**My Contributions:**

- Conducted market research and target audience analysis.
- Contributed to marketing strategy development.
- Identified target customer segments, including solo travelers and families.
- Worked on marketing ideas to increase user engagement.
- Collaborated with a team of four members.

**Key Features:**

- AI-powered travel guidance.
- Augmented Reality (AR) experiences.
- Interactive puzzles and challenges.
- Gamification, points, and digital badges.
- Engaging cultural and historical experiences.
